FACTS THE DESIGN MUST RESPECT
- A real reading takes about 77-90 s with Qwen3.8-27B (median about 80 s; both Stage 3 runs) and about 35 s with Gemma-4-31B-IT (median 30 s; Stage 5 run), sometimes more. Vercel functions on this project (Hobby) have maxDuration 300 s (docs/sources/vercel-limits.md). Instances do not share memory (OI-S2-01).
- Token Factory: one key for the team; about 20 requests per minute per account, 8 concurrent, a rolling daily cap of 1 USD of free credits (about 0.0002 per reading), 429 and 5xx happen, prompts are kept about 2 days by the provider.
- The deployed site must never present a stored result as live (I7), never put the key in the browser, and never let anonymous traffic spend the quota.

STAGE 6 — DELIVER
