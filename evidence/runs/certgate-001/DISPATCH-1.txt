PRODUCT INVARIANTS (every stage; the auditor rejects a candidate that breaks any of them)
I1. Decisions come only from deterministic, versioned rules. The model reads and explains; it never decides. A model explanation without a reference to verifiable input never becomes a fact.
I2. A field that cannot be read with confidence is unknown, never an invented value. A critical field that is unknown forces review.
I3. Two reading channels, not two identical readings: for a PDF with a text layer, the text layer (extracted without any model) is compared with the model reading; disagreement means unknown plus review. A scan without a text layer has no second channel: critical fields require human confirmation, and the UI says so.
I4. Exact decimals for every comparison; no binary floating point anywhere in rules, coherence or units.
I5. No proposal of release when: evidence is missing, channels disagree, heat or lot does not match the delivery note, a rule fails, units are inconsistent, the inspection stamp is missing, or the model output is invalid.
I6. No secrets in the repository, the room, logs or evidence. Configuration by environment variables; .env.example without values; .env ignored.
I7. A recorded response is never presented as a live reading. Every screen that shows a stored result labels it as stored, with its timestamp.
